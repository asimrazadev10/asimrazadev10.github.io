Privacy Policy for RozTool

Last updated: October 24, 2026
Developer: Asim Raza

RozTool is an all-in-one toolbox for documents, PDFs, photos, scanning, QR codes, writing and everyday tools. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Data processed on your device

Most tools work locally on your device. Files you select — PDFs, photos, scans — and text you enter are processed on the device and are not sent to us.

What we do not collect

RozTool does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, or contact list through the app.

Internet-dependent services

Some features need internet and have their own data practices: advertising services, network tools (such as currency reference rates), downloaded on-device AI models, and some system speech services. These services may collect device or usage information as described in their own privacy policies.

Usage analytics (on by default, opt-out in Settings)

RozTool includes Firebase Analytics, and analytics collection is enabled by default so we can see which tools are useful and what to improve. The statistics are anonymous usage data — such as which tools are opened and completed, and ad interactions — sent to Google Firebase Analytics. Your files, documents, and tool contents are never included. You can opt out at any time: open Settings → Privacy and turn off the "Analytics" switch — collection stops immediately and your choice is remembered. See Google's privacy policy for how Firebase Analytics data is handled.

Advertising

RozTool shows labelled ads. An optional rewarded ad can pause banners and automatic full-screen ads on this device for 30 minutes after the reward is earned. Declining an ad never blocks a tool or export. Ads are served by Google AdMob; see Google's privacy policy for how ad-related data is handled.

Your choices

  • QR history is saved only when you choose to save it.
  • Feedback is never sent by the app itself: feedback drafts stay on your device and open as a draft in your own email app, addressed to us, so you can review everything before sending. No third-party feedback service receives your feedback.
  • Usage analytics is on by default; you can opt out at any time in Settings → Privacy with the "Analytics" switch, and it will stay off.
  • Deadline reminders are delivered by local notifications; delivery depends on your system notification settings and may be approximate.

Data retention

Because your files and personal information are processed on your device and are not sent to us, we hold no account data to retain or delete. Data handled by third-party services is retained under their own policies.

Data security and children

Local processing keeps your files on your device. RozTool is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version will always be available at this URL.
